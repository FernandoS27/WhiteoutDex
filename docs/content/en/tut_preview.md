# Tutorial: Preview your model

3ds Max cannot show a model exactly like the game: the game has its own light, shaders, team color and effects. The **WhiteoutFlakes** previewer can.

**You need:** the scene from the lesson *Import a model*.

## Step 1: Open the previewer

Choose **WhiteoutDex > [[t:menu_renderer]]**. A new window opens and shows the model.

![The WhiteoutFlakes previewer next to 3ds Max 2016.](media_2.png)

## Step 2: Choose an animation

1. In the toolbar of the previewer, open the **Animation** list.
2. Choose *Walk*. The Paladin walks.
3. Choose *Free Range*. Now the previewer follows the time slider of 3ds Max. Move the slider and watch.

## Step 3: Change the look

1. Click the **team color** box and choose another color. The cape changes its color.
2. Under **Lighting** choose *Glue*. This is the light of the game menus.
3. Under **View > Warcraft III Art** choose *Classic* or *Reforged (HD)* to compare both looks.

## Step 4: Change the model and see it at once

1. In 3ds Max, select a mesh and move it a little.
2. Look at the previewer: it shows the change right away.
3. Undo the change with [[key:Ctrl+Z]].

!!! tip
    Click **Resync** in the previewer after you add or delete objects. It reads the whole scene again.
