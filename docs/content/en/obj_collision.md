# Collision Shapes

Collision shapes tell the game where the player can **click** the model. They are invisible in the game. Units usually have spheres, buildings a box.

Create them with **Create > Helpers > Warcraft III Plugins > Wc3 CollisionSphere** or **Wc3 CollisionBox**. Link them to the bone that should move them.

## Both shapes

[[shot:obj_collision_sphere_r1|General Features.]]

| No. | What it does |
|---|---|
| [[ctl:3]] | Click this, then click an object. The shape jumps to that object and links to it. |
