## The Warcraft III objects

WhiteoutDex adds new objects to the **Create** panel of 3ds Max:

- **Create > Helpers > Warcraft III Plugins**: attachment points, collision shapes, lights, event objects, Popcorn FX, FaceFX.
- **Create > Geometry > Particle Systems**: Particle Emitter 1, Particle Emitter 2 and Ribbon Emitter.
- **Material Editor**: the material *Wc3Material* and the map *Wc3Bitmap*.
- **Modifier list**: *Wc3 Vertex Color*.

The chapters *Warcraft III objects* and *Materials* explain them.

## Your first steps

1. Open [[t:menu_settings]] and set your game folder (see *Settings*).
2. Do the tutorial *Import a model*.
3. Look at the model in the previewer: tutorial *Preview your model*.
4. Export it again: tutorial *Export a model*.
