# Anim: Model Scaler

Scales the whole model, with its bones, animations and special objects. Use it when a model is too big or too small for the game.

Open it with **WhiteoutDex > [[t:menu_anim_scaler]]**.

[[shot:model_scaler|The Model Scaler.]]

| No. | What it does |
|---|---|
| [[ctl:4]] | A target height from the game, for example *Footman* or *Townhall* (classic or Reforged). |
| [[ctl:6]] | Scales the model to the chosen height. |
| [[ctl:10]] | Your own factor. 2 makes the model twice as big, 0.5 half as big. |
| [[ctl:11]] | Scales the model by your factor. |

!!! warning
    Models with a **Biped** or **CAT** rig and a Skin modifier do not scale well this way. WhiteoutDex warns you. Scale the rig with its own tools instead.

!!! tip
    Save your scene before you scale. Then you can go back if the result is not right.
